    Readings

    These are the primary sources behind this page and others worth opening directly.

    On this map

    • Google News, BBC and Al Jazeera feeds for headlines, placed by the location named in the headline
    • adsb.lol for military aircraft that broadcast their position
    • USGS for earthquakes of magnitude 4.5 and above
    • NASA EONET for wildfires, storms and volcanoes
    • CISA KEV for software flaws under active attack
    • Ransomware.live for victims posted on leak sites
    • NOAA Space Weather for the geomagnetic Kp index
    • Yahoo Finance for oil, gas, gold and volatility quotes

    Conflict

    • Liveuamap geolocated incident reports
    • ISW daily campaign assessments
    • DeepStateMap the Ukrainian front line
    • NASA FIRMS satellite heat detections of strikes and fires
    • ACLED coded conflict events

    Air, sea and signals

    • ADS-B Exchange unfiltered aircraft tracking
    • GPSJam daily GPS interference map
    • IMF PortWatch chokepoint transit counts
    • UKMTO shipping attack warnings
    • NGA maritime warnings sea closures before missile tests

    Nuclear

    • IAEA inspection and plant safety updates
    • Bulletin of the Atomic Scientists the Doomsday Clock
    • Arms Control Association treaties and missile tests
    • 38 North North Korean site imagery

    Cyber and internet

    • Cloudflare Radar outages and attack traffic
    • IODA internet shutdowns by country
    • NetBlocks confirmed blackouts
    • GreyNoise mass-scanning surges

    AI, grid and early warning

    • AI Incident Database documented harms
    • METR capability evaluations
    • DOE OE-417 US grid disturbance reports
    • State Department advisories embassy drawdowns
    • Polymarket crowd odds on escalation

    Headlines are unverified and placed automatically, so a pin shows where a story is about, not an exact location. Aircraft that switch off their transponders do not appear.
